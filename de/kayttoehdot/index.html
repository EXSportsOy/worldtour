6. Conduct, nicknames and restrictions

Treat other players, testers, support staff and moderators respectfully. These rules cover nicknames and content displayed in the game and its online services, and game-related communication with EXSports support and in community channels operated by EXSports, where those features are available.

6.1 Disruptive behaviour and prohibited content

  • Bullying, targeted personal abuse, repeated unwanted contact, stalking, threats and incitement to violence are prohibited.
  • Hate speech, racism, discriminatory or sexual harassment, and sexually offensive or child-inappropriate content are prohibited.
  • Unlawfully disclosing another person's personal information, impersonating another person or staff, scams and phishing are prohibited.
  • Spam, repeated message flooding, unauthorised advertising, encouraging others to harass someone, and knowingly false reports intended to harm another person are prohibited. A good-faith report is not a violation merely because the suspicion proves unfounded.
  • Deliberately overloading or disrupting the service, unauthorised access, manipulating competitive results, and evading restrictions through new accounts or someone else's account are prohibited.

Respectful criticism, negative feedback, bug reports, requests for remedies and exercising privacy rights are not disruptive behaviour. Disagreement with EXSports alone is not grounds for a restriction. Report security vulnerabilities privately to info@exsports.fi; do not exploit them to access other people's data.

6.2 Nicknames

A nickname must not contain prohibited content described above, abusive or vulgar language, sexual references, threats or messages degrading other users. It must not misrepresent identity or status, such as by pretending to be an EXSports employee, moderator or another player. Do not include your full name, contact details or another person's personal information. Unauthorised advertising and names infringing others' rights are prohibited.

The rules apply in all languages and to attempts to evade them using numbers, symbols, spacing or alternative spellings. Meaning and context are considered when assessing a nickname. EXSports may require an inappropriate nickname to be changed, hide it or replace it with a neutral name. Nickname changes may be restricted where needed to prevent abuse.

6.3 Action and review

Action is proportionate to the seriousness, repetition and impact of the violation. It may include a reminder or warning, correcting, hiding or removing content or a nickname, rejecting a score, or temporarily restricting online features. Serious or repeated violations may result in a permanent online ban or closure of the player account. A serious threat, disclosure of personal information or another situation requiring immediate protection may result in an immediate restriction without prior warning.

EXSports may end an individual threatening or abusive support conversation and direct further communication to a written channel. This does not remove the right to complain, seek remedies or exercise statutory rights. An online sanction does not by itself remove rights to separately purchased offline content or mandatory consumer rights.

Report inappropriate conduct or nicknames to info@exsports.fi with relevant details, such as the nickname, result URL and a description of what happened. The number of reports alone does not establish a violation. We explain restriction decisions, reasons and the available review route unless law or a justified security need prevents disclosure of particular information. You may request human reconsideration at the same address and provide your account of events. Statutory appeal and dispute rights remain unaffected. Also see the World Rank rules.
