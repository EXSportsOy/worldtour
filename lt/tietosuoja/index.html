Privacy notice · 23 October 2026. This version covers the game released on 1 December 2026: the Google Play version, the player account (section 9), the one-time purchase (section 10) and World Rank (section 11), as well as separately distributed playtest builds. The Google Play version does not send playtest telemetry. The backup details for the playtest (section 5) are still being verified before this version is adopted.

1. Controller

EXSports Oy, Finnish Business ID 2719500-4, Korkeavuorenkatu 25, 48100 Kotka, Finland. Privacy and other enquiries: info@exsports.fi. Company details and telephone. This notice covers the EXS World Tour Android game (fi.exsports.exsworldtour), its player account, one-time purchase and World Rank, playtest builds and worldtour.exsports.fi.

2. Data processed by the game

  • On your device: settings, language, tutorial and career progress, personal results and replays, and technical logs. The Google Play version does not send these to us.
  • Google Play version: does not collect or send playtest telemetry and does not show the playtest acceptance screen. The game only contacts our server for the player account (section 9), the one-time purchase (section 10) and World Rank (section 11).
  • Test data sent to the server by playtest builds: after you accept, separately distributed test builds send a random installation identifier and session identifier, timestamps and game version; jump results and tricks, attempts and progress, settings and language, performance measurements, warnings and crash logs. Local logs may be created before you accept participation.
  • Device information in playtest builds: manufacturer, model, operating system, processor architecture, memory and core count, graphics hardware, refresh rate, screen resolution and density.
  • Test invitations: if you register with us for testing, the contact details you provide and the Google account email needed to manage test access. That address is not added to the game's automatic telemetry uploads.
  • Support requests: the email address and message you provide, and additional information needed to handle the issue.

The playtest installation identifier is not your name, but sessions with the same identifier can be linked. This is pseudonymous rather than fully anonymous data. When the game or website connects to a server, network infrastructure processes the connection's IP address. The game does not request location, contacts, camera or microphone access and contains no ads or advertising-network tracking.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Local game data enables play and stores progress. Identifiable test logs from playtest builds help fix faults, assess performance and controls, and balance the game. Personal data is not used for targeted advertising or sold. The purposes and legal bases for the player account, one-time purchase and World Rank are set out in sections 9–11.

The legal basis for playtest telemetry is separate consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). Taking part in a playtest is voluntary: you can leave the test and use the Google Play version, which sends no test data. A playtest build asks for your acceptance before the first upload. You can withdraw it in settings with Delete playtest data, which also deletes the data the installation has already sent. Acceptance of game terms does not replace that consent.

Support uses information necessary for contractual matters to perform the contract (6(1)(b)); other enquiries and necessary security rely on legitimate interests in handling communications and protecting the service (6(1)(f)). Information required by law is processed to comply with that obligation (6(1)(c)). The basis and necessity are assessed by data category; wanting to improve a game does not justify unlimited collection.

Sending your requested test invitation and managing test access rely on our legitimate interest in organising a closed test (6(1)(f)). Google Play processes the tester group in its service. You can end participation and request removal from the group by email; without the address needed for test access, an individual invitation cannot be provided.

4. Providers and transfers

Playtest builds send technical logs to Google's Firebase Realtime Database (region europe-west1). Earlier test versions sent them to Supabase (Ireland region); that database was deleted on 23 October 2026 and the data no longer exists. The providers process the data on EXSports' behalf. The website is delivered through Firebase Hosting; Google processes visitors' IP addresses, among other information, to deliver the site and keep it secure.

Providers may process information outside the EU/EEA even when the primary database is in the EU. Transfers to the United States rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, to which Google LLC is certified, and on the Standard Contractual Clauses in the Google Cloud Data Processing Addendum. You may request information about safeguards by email.

Provider details: Privacy and Security in Firebase and the Google Cloud Data Processing Addendum. Providers for the player account, one-time purchase and World Rank (Firebase Authentication, Google Cloud, Google Play and Play Integrity) are described in sections 9–11.

5. Retention and deletion

The company has approved a maximum retention of 90 days after the relevant test period ends for identifiable test data. Testers will be informed when testing ends. Unnecessary data is deleted earlier. Where an earlier acceptance screen promised retention only during testing, that shorter promise applies; the new maximum does not retrospectively extend it.

Deletion covers identifiable test datasets and copies controlled by EXSports. No backups are taken of the player account, purchase and World Rank database, and deleted data disappears from the service after the version history of at most one hour. Automatic backups have not been enabled for the playtest database; this is verified before this version is adopted. Truly anonymised statistics may be retained if they can no longer be linked to a person or installation.

Test invitation contacts are retained for test access and necessary test communications, no longer than the test-data limit above. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve the issue and necessary legal claims. Any legally required transaction records are retained for the applicable statutory period. If data must remain following a deletion request, we explain the scope and reason. Device saves remain until you remove them or the app; this does not delete information already sent to the server.

The Google Play version sends no test data. If the device still holds the installation identifier of an earlier playtest build, Delete playtest data in settings also deletes, in the Google Play version, the data that installation sent from the server and the device. If the game has been uninstalled or its data cleared in between, the identifier no longer exists; in that case, request deletion by email (section 6). Retention for the player account, one-time purchase and World Rank is described in sections 9–11.

6. Your rights and requests

Subject to applicable conditions, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction and portability of your data. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw consent for future processing. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

Email info@exsports.fi. See the practical deletion instructions, including if you do not have an account. We respond without undue delay, generally within one month. If a legally permitted extension of up to two additional months is necessary, we explain the reason within the first month.

You may complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman or another competent supervisory authority. World Rank attempts are checked automatically (section 11), but this has no legal or similarly significant effects within the meaning of the GDPR. A person always decides on rejecting a result for cheating or hiding a nickname, and you can ask for the decision to be reviewed.

7. Children

The game is intended for people aged 16 or over. Only people aged 16 or over can create a player account and take part in World Rank (section 9). Playtests are open to people aged 16 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from younger children. If a child has used the service contrary to the age limit, a guardian can contact us to investigate and request deletion.

8. Website and future features

The website has no analytics or advertising tracking and no cookies set by EXSports. On the account page and the account deletion page you sign in with your Google account, and Firebase then manages the sign-in session in your browser (section 9). See cookies and technical information. An email link opens your own email application.

The player account and Google sign-in are described in section 9, the one-time purchase in section 10 and World Rank in section 11. The website leaderboard shows the official World Rank results once a season has started. The game has no weekly contests or videos. New features and processing purposes will be explained in this notice before launch, with separate consent where required.

9. Player account and Google sign-in

A player account is optional, and the game can be played without one. An account may be created by a person aged 16 or over who lives in an EU or EEA country, Switzerland, the United States, Canada, Australia or New Zealand. Sign-in is only available with a Google account. Account processing only starts when you create an account.

  • Sign-in: Google and Firebase Authentication process your Google account identifier, email address, name and profile picture as well as your IP address and device and browser information during sign-in. We only request basic information from Google (openid, email, profile). We do not copy your email address, name or picture into our own database or show them to others.
  • Our account database: user identifier, nickname and its comparison form, account status, creation and change times, the accepted terms version and when it was accepted, and the fact that you meet the age limit together with the version of the age policy used. Your date of birth is neither sent nor stored; it is only checked on your device.
  • Security: request receipts and rate limits to prevent misuse and duplicate requests, and server error and security logs without nicknames or sign-in tokens.
  • On your device: the sign-in session. On Android it is stored encrypted in the device keystore and is excluded from backups; in a browser, Firebase manages the session. Signing out removes the session.

Providing the account is based on contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): we need this data to create the account, sign you in and manage your nickname. Security data is based on our legitimate interest in protecting the service and its users (Art. 6(1)(f)). Account data is not used for advertising or profiling, and playtest telemetry is not linked to the account. Your nickname is only shown publicly if you take part in World Rank (section 11).

The account database and server run on Google Cloud in the europe-west1 region (Belgium). Firebase Authentication also processes data in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, under which Google is certified, and on the standard contractual clauses in the Google Cloud data processing terms.

Account data is kept for as long as the account exists. You can delete your account at any time at worldtour.exsports.fi/delete-account or by email; see the deletion guide. A deletion request blocks the account immediately. Our account data and the Firebase Authentication user are deleted as soon as possible and within 30 days at the latest. The nickname is detached from the account immediately, and its comparison form stays reserved for 30 days so that nobody can take over the name right away. Receipts and rate limits are removed within 30 days at the latest. The effect of deletion on the one-time purchase and World Rank data is described in sections 10 and 11. No backups or exports are taken of the account database, so deleted data does not remain in them; if backups are introduced later, deletion will cover them before they are enabled and this notice will be updated.

Google states that it deletes Firebase Authentication data from its internal systems and backups within 180 days of deletion and keeps IP addresses in logs for a few weeks (Firebase privacy information). Deleting your account does not delete your Google account or the game progress stored on your device.

10. One-time purchase and entitlements

The Unlock full game one-time purchase is made in Google Play. Google processes the payment, payment method and your Google account details under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your name, email address or payment card details. No player account is needed to make the purchase.

  • Purchase check: the game sends the Google Play purchase token to our server. The server checks the purchase status with Google Play and returns a signed entitlement. The purchase token is not written to logs or stored on your device.
  • Purchase record: a hash calculated from the purchase token, the product, the granted entitlement and any Season 1 bonus, the rules version, the payment completion time reported by Google Play, the purchase status (such as pending, valid or cancelled) and check times. The record contains no player account, email address, Google Play account name or device identifier.
  • Temporary data: the purchase token in encrypted form for up to 48 hours so that the purchase can be acknowledged to Google Play; hashes of Google Play purchase and cancellation notifications for 35 days; and, to prevent misuse, hashes calculated from your IP address and purchase token, which expire after one hour.
  • On your device: the entitlement signed by our server, so that purchased content also works offline. It contains no purchase token and is excluded from device backups. On a new device, the purchase is restored from Google Play with Restore purchases.

This processing is based on contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): we need the data to deliver and restore the purchased content and to handle cancellations. Preventing misuse is based on our legitimate interest in protecting the service (Art. 6(1)(f)). The purchase record is stored on Google Cloud in the europe-west1 region (Belgium). Google Play also processes data in its own service in the United States. If you join World Rank, a hash of your purchase is bound permanently to your first World Rank account (section 11).

The purchase record is kept for as long as we offer the purchased content and its restoration, and then for three years to deal with possible legal claims. We keep Google Play sales reports as accounting records under the Finnish Accounting Act (Art. 6(1)(c)); they contain no data about individual buyers. Deleting your player account does not delete or cancel the purchase. You can ask us by email to delete purchase data; we will then explain how deletion affects restoring the purchase.

11. World Rank

World Rank is an optional seasonal competition included in the full game. Taking part requires a player account (section 9) and the full game, meaning the one-time purchase (section 10). Data is only sent once you have joined the season and selected a World Rank attempt before your run; otherwise your results stay on your device. Anyone can view the leaderboard without an account.

  • Joining a season and purchase binding: when you first join, a hash of your purchase is bound permanently to your player account so that one purchase can be used with one account. We also record whether support has moved the purchase to another account (at most once) and how many purchases bound to your account have been revoked. A second revoked purchase closes World Rank for the account. At the end of a season we check that the purchase is still valid, because only valid purchases count towards final placements and rewards. The purchase token is kept encrypted only for the competition session, up to about 10 hours, and is removed when you sign out.
  • Attempts: user identifier, season, slope, stance, rules versions, server timestamps, a checksum of the recording, the attempt status and any rejection reason, and the official and exact score.
  • Input recording: the control inputs of your run, not video, images or audio. The server recalculates the score from the inputs. The recording is kept private and is never published.
  • Device check: when you submit, Google Play Integrity checks that the game was installed from Google Play and has not been modified, and that the device meets Google's integrity requirements. For this check Google processes app information, the Play licence status and device attestation data under its own terms and deletes them after the fixed retention period it states. We do not send Google your nickname or player account identifier; the request only includes a hash calculated from the attempt. We use Google's result only to accept or reject the attempt and do not store it.
  • Public leaderboard: nickname, official score and placement on each slope. The leaderboard is visible to everyone in the game and on the website, also without signing in. User identifiers, input recordings and exact scores are not published. When a season ends, the top 100 on each slope are saved with their nicknames in the season archive.
  • Rewards: in-game season rewards, any corrections to them and related notices are stored with your account. Your device keeps a reward cache that is excluded from backups and removed when you sign out.
  • Reports, blocks and moderation: you can report a leaderboard row to the moderators in the game (reason: nickname or cheating) or by email. A report stores your user identifier, the reported row and the reason. You can also block a player, which hides their nickname from you only. Moderation decisions (rejecting a result, hiding a nickname) are recorded with their reasons. A report never leads to an automatic sanction; a person always makes the decision.

Running the competition, checking attempts, leaderboards and rewards are based on contract (Art. 6(1)(b)), meaning the terms of use and the World Rank rules. Preventing cheating, moderation and the season archive are based on our legitimate interest in running a fair competition and keeping season results (Art. 6(1)(f)). You can object to your nickname being shown in the season archive and ask us to hide it at info@exsports.fi. Hiding it does not change placements. The data is stored on Google Cloud in the europe-west1 region (Belgium).

We keep World Rank data as follows:

  • Input recordings: 120 days after the attempt is checked.
  • Rejected results: 190 days after rejection.
  • Reports: 190 days after the report is made.
  • Blocks: until you remove the block or either account is deleted.
  • Attempt data, purchase binding, number of revoked purchases and rewards: for as long as your account exists.
  • Season archive with nicknames and accepted results: five years from the end of the season. After that, nicknames and account identifiers are removed from the archive, leaving an anonymous results history. Your nickname is removed from the archive earlier when you delete your account, and you can ask us to hide it at any time.
  • Moderation decisions: five years from the decision, so that correction requests and disputes can be handled.

When you delete your player account, we delete your attempts, input recordings, rewards and reward notices, and reports and blocks made by you or about you, within 30 days at the latest. Your nickname is removed from the leaderboard and from every archive version. An anonymous place remains in the archive so that other players' placements do not change, and the scores of accepted results remain on the server without any link to your account. Your account identifier is removed from the purchase binding, and the purchase is released from the start of the next season. If you delete your account during a season, the same purchase cannot be used with another account in that season.

12. Additional information for players outside the European Union

The game is available in EU and EEA countries, Switzerland, the United States, Canada, Australia and New Zealand. We process all players' data in accordance with this notice, and the same level of protection as under the EU General Data Protection Regulation applies to everyone. Account, purchase and World Rank data is stored in Belgium (europe-west1).

  • United States: we do not sell or share personal information with third parties for money or other valuable consideration, and we do not use it for targeted advertising. The game contains no ads. The service is not directed to children under 13, and a player account cannot be created by anyone under 16. You can request access to, correction of or deletion of your data at info@exsports.fi, and making a request will not affect how you are treated.
  • Canada and Quebec: the person in charge of the protection of personal information at EXSports Oy (in Quebec, the responsable de la protection des renseignements personnels) is the Managing Director, who can be reached at info@exsports.fi. Your data is processed outside Canada, in the EU and the United States (sections 4 and 9–11). We keep a record of privacy breaches and report them as required by law. Residents of Quebec may also contact us in French and may lodge a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.
  • Switzerland: we apply the rights under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection in the same way as the rights under the GDPR. You may also lodge a complaint with the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).
  • Australia and New Zealand: you can request access to and correction of your data at info@exsports.fi. You may also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) or the New Zealand Privacy Commissioner. If a privacy breach affecting players in New Zealand is likely to cause serious harm, we notify the New Zealand Privacy Commissioner and the affected players as required by law.

Your data is transferred from your country of residence to the EU. Google's services also process data in the United States; providers and transfer mechanisms are described in sections 4 and 9–11.
