Account and account deletion

The world ranking account is created with a Google account in the game or on this site when the test season starts. You can delete the account from the game's settings or from this page after signing in. Deletion removes the nickname, results, replays and videos.

Sign-in and account deletion will open here when the service is live. Until then, send requests to: info@exsports.fi
EXS World Tour is a game by EXSports Oy for Android. The S and M slopes are free; the L slope is a one-time purchase of €1.95.
