How it works

Full guide
1 · In-run

Pop off the lip

Crouch in the in-run, pop at the lip. Timing decides height, and height gives you time for the trick.

2 · Air

Spin and grab

Spin and flip on their own sliders, grab with a button: Nose, Tail, Indy or Weddle. Hold the grab for at least 0.2 seconds and let go before the snow.

3 · Landing

Land it clean

Keep the board straight and finish the rotation before landing. A clean landing applies a score multiplier; a crash resets the score to zero.
