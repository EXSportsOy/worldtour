World ranking

Opens 1 Dec 2026

    World Rank opens with Season 1 on 1 Dec 2026. The list fills up once the first verified results come in.

    Your bests

    Season 1 starts 1 Dec 2026

    Your results appear on the list once World Rank opens on 1 Dec 2026. Taking part requires the full game and a free player account (Google sign-in, age 16+).

    About accounts

    Rules in brief

    • One best result per player on each slope.
    • The server recalculates every score from the player's inputs. The score and its breakdown appear on the website, the replay in the game.
    • Only the Android game installed from Google Play qualifies.
    • Test-season results will be reset and hidden at game launch. Any archive for production seasons will be announced separately.
