Guide

How to play

The game starts with a tutorial that teaches the run, the pop, the grab and the first tricks in seven steps. The tutorial uses the S and M kickers, and the career track opens after it.

Getting started

  1. Choose Tutorial from the main menu.
  2. Pick the current exercise and read Do this and Why?.
  3. Watch example shows the inputs for the same task. Watching does not count as a completion.
  4. Start the exercise and press Go on the slope. In steps 5–7, set the flip or spin before you go.
  5. A success is saved before the next task. Completed tasks can be repeated.

Controls

  • Crouch in the in-run: the slider on the left, the fill shows how deep you are.
  • Pop near the lip. Your timing shows as feedback in the air; a perfect pop is PERFECT.
  • Spin and flip on their own sliders. Direction FS or BS, amount in 180° steps.
  • Grabs Nose, Tail, Indy and Weddle are buttons during the air. A grab needs a hold of at least 0.2 seconds, and the hold time affects rotation.
  • Landing is Clean, Sketchy or a crash. Let go of the grab before the snow.

In Play mode every jump starts with the sliders reset. The rider sets off as soon as you touch any control on the start screen.

The seven tutorial steps

Career track

After the tutorial, Play opens the career. The four S tasks unlock the M slope, and the six M tasks unlock the L tasks.

Small S4 tasks · free
Medium M6 tasks · free
Large L10 tasks · €1.95

World ranking

When a result is ready for the ranking, submit it from the result card in the game. The server recomputes the score from your inputs, and the verified result appears on the world ranking under your nickname.
