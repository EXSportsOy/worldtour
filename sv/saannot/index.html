World Rank in brief · 23 October 2026. This page is a summary. The binding World Rank rules are in section 9 of the terms of use (version 1.4), which you accept with your player account terms. If they differ, the terms of use prevail.

1. Participation

World Rank is a free feature of the full version. You need the Unlock the full game one-time purchase, a player account (Google sign-in, age 16 or over), and the game installed from Google Play on a device that passes the Play Integrity check. Joining for the first time permanently binds the purchase to your account. Details in sections 9.2 and 9.3.

2. Seasons and rewards

Season 1 runs from 1 December 2026 00:00 to 1 March 2027 00:00 UTC. There are three lists (S, M and L) with one best score per player and slope. Rewards are in-game outfit items: a season memento for every participant and badges for the three highest scores on each slope. Weekly competitions are not part of the release. Sections 9.1, 9.6 and 9.7.

3. Fair play and verification

The server recalculates every score from your control inputs. Submit only runs you played yourself. Modified games, bots, macros and altered recordings are prohibited. Sections 9.4, 9.5 and 9.8.

4. Nickname

Your nickname is public. Do not use your full name, contact details or abusive names. Section 6 of the terms of use.

5. Report a problem

In the game: open the World Rank list, select another player's row and choose Report or Block. By email: info@exsports.fi. We aim to handle reports within seven days. Section 9.9.

6. Moderation and review

A report never leads to an automatic sanction. A person decides and records the reason, and you can ask for reconsideration. Sections 6.3 and 9.9.

7. Public content and your data

Only your nickname, official score and placement are shown on the list. Input recordings and videos are not published. Account deletion and retention are explained in section 9.10 and the privacy notice. Organiser: EXSports Oy, company details.
