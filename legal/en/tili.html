EXS World Tour · delete data and accounts · updated 25 October 2026. You can delete your player account yourself on the page below. Other deletion requests can be made by email without reinstalling the game or signing in.

Request deletion

If the button does not open email, write to info@exsports.fi with the subject “EXS World Tour – delete data or account”. You may also use this channel to withdraw consent or exercise other privacy rights.

If you have a player account, you can also delete it yourself: sign in with Google at worldtour.exsports.fi/delete-account and confirm the deletion. The page does not ask for your age, a nickname or acceptance of new terms.

1. Make a request

  1. Say that your request concerns EXS World Tour and whether you want playtest data, your player account, or both deleted.
  2. If the request concerns playtest data, provide the game version, device model and approximate test dates so we can help locate your data. These details alone do not prove identity.
  3. If the request concerns a player account, provide the nickname and, where possible, email us from the address linked to that account.
  4. Do not send a Google password, payment card details, sign-in code or copy of an identity document in ordinary email.

We may request reasonable additional information to avoid deleting someone else's data. The current random installation identifier is not easily visible in the game; we will help establish a safe way to match the request. If we cannot link data to you, we explain this. We do not collect unnecessary personal information solely to identify you.

2. What is deleted

From the playtest, we delete identifiable test logs and identifier-linked data reliably matched to the request, except where a lawful retention requirement justifies a limited exception. We explain any exception and retention period. Truly anonymised statistics no longer relate to you.

Player account deletion covers the EXSports player account, nickname, World Rank attempts and input recordings, rewards, reports and blocks, and other personal data linked to the account (privacy notice, sections 9 and 11). It does not delete your Google account or Google Play's own purchase records, and it does not cancel the purchase. Deleting an account is not itself a refund request.

3. Response time and local data

We respond without undue delay, generally within one month. Any legally permitted extension and its reason will be notified within the first month. We confirm deletion and explain any information that must remain.

Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage in Android settings removes local information and may erase progress. It does not delete data already uploaded. To stop playtest build uploads immediately, choose Delete playtest data in the settings, or close the game and do not reopen it until support advises you. Sending an email does not technically stop an uploader already running on your device.

4. Deleting a player account

You can delete your account yourself at worldtour.exsports.fi/delete-account by signing in with the same Google account. Deletion does not require accepting new terms or having a nickname. The request blocks the account immediately, and account data is deleted within 30 days at the latest as described in the privacy notice. Email remains an alternative for people who cannot sign in; we then verify account ownership before deletion.
