1. Controller

EXSports Oy, Finnish Business ID 2719500-4, Korkeavuorenkatu 25, 48100 Kotka, Finland. Privacy and other enquiries: info@exsports.fi. Company details and telephone. This notice covers the EXS World Tour Android game (fi.exsports.exsworldtour), its playtest and worldtour.exsports.fi.

2. Data in the current playtest

  • On your device: settings, language, tutorial and career progress, personal results and replays, and technical logs. Local logs may be created before you accept participation.
  • Test data sent to the server: a random installation identifier and session identifier, timestamps and game version; jump results and tricks, attempts and progress, settings and language, performance measurements, warnings and crash logs.
  • Device information: manufacturer, model, operating system, processor architecture, memory and core count, graphics hardware, refresh rate, screen resolution and density. Network infrastructure also processes the connection's IP address.
  • Test invitations: if you register with us for testing, the contact details you provide and the Google account email needed to manage test access. That address is not added to the game's automatic telemetry uploads.
  • Support requests: the email address and message you provide, and additional information needed to handle the issue.

The installation identifier is not your name, but sessions with the same identifier can be linked. This is pseudonymous rather than fully anonymous data. The current game does not request location, contacts, camera or microphone access and contains no advertising-network tracking. No player account is created in the current test.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Local game data enables play and stores progress. Identifiable test logs help fix faults, assess performance and controls, and balance the game. They are not used for targeted advertising or sold to advertisers.

The intended legal basis for test analytics is separate consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). The current test build only permits starting through an acceptance screen; it does not yet offer play without uploads or withdrawal in settings. Correcting this flow and ensuring freely given consent are prerequisites for adopting this notice. Acceptance of game terms does not replace that consent.

Support uses information necessary for contractual matters to perform the contract (6(1)(b)); other enquiries and necessary security rely on legitimate interests in handling communications and protecting the service (6(1)(f)). Information required by law is processed to comply with that obligation (6(1)(c)). The basis and necessity are assessed by data category; wanting to improve a game does not justify unlimited collection.

Sending your requested test invitation and managing test access rely on our legitimate interest in organising a closed test (6(1)(f)). Google Play processes the tester group in its service. You can end participation and request removal from the group by email; without the address needed for test access, an individual invitation cannot be provided.

4. Providers and transfers

The current test sends technical logs to Google's Firebase Realtime Database (region europe-west1). Earlier test versions sent them to Supabase (documented in Ireland). The providers process the data on EXSports' behalf. The cloud configuration, agreements and backup locations must be confirmed before this draft is adopted. The website is delivered through Firebase Hosting; Google processes visitors' IP addresses, among other information, to deliver the site and keep it secure.

Providers may process information outside the EU/EEA even when the primary database is in the EU. We therefore do not promise exclusively EU-based processing. The final notice must confirm the applicable transfer mechanism, such as an adequacy decision or EU Standard Contractual Clauses with any necessary additional safeguards. You may request information about safeguards by email.

Provider details: Privacy and Security in Firebase, the Google Cloud Data Processing Addendum and the Supabase Data Processing Addendum. For any future purchase, Google Play handles payment and account information under its own terms; EXSports does not receive your full payment card number.

5. Retention and deletion

The company has approved a maximum retention of 90 days after the relevant test period ends for identifiable test data. Testers will be informed when testing ends. Unnecessary data is deleted earlier. Where an earlier acceptance screen promised retention only during testing, that shorter promise applies; the new maximum does not retrospectively extend it.

Deletion covers identifiable test datasets and copies controlled by EXSports. Backup expiry still needs verification; this notice does not claim automated deletion is already implemented. Truly anonymised statistics may be retained if they can no longer be linked to a person or installation.

Test invitation contacts are retained for test access and necessary test communications, no longer than the test-data limit above. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve the issue and necessary legal claims. Any legally required transaction records are retained for the applicable statutory period. If data must remain following a deletion request, we explain the scope and reason. Device saves remain until you remove them or the app; this does not delete information already sent to the server.

6. Your rights and requests

Subject to applicable conditions, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction and portability of your data. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw consent for future processing. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

Email info@exsports.fi. See the practical deletion instructions, including if you do not have an account. We respond without undue delay, generally within one month. If a legally permitted extension of up to two additional months is necessary, we explain the reason within the first month.

You may complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman or another competent supervisory authority. The current test does not make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects within the meaning of the GDPR.

9. Player account and Google sign-in

A player account is optional, and the game can be played without one. An account may be created by a person aged 16 or over who lives in an EU or EEA country, Switzerland, the United States, Canada, Australia or New Zealand. Sign-in is only available with a Google account. Account processing only starts when you create an account; registration of new accounts opens separately.

  • Sign-in: Google and Firebase Authentication process your Google account identifier, email address, name and profile picture as well as your IP address and device and browser information during sign-in. We only request basic information from Google (openid, email, profile). We do not copy your email address, name or picture into our own database or show them to others.
  • Our account database: user identifier, nickname and its comparison form, account status, creation and change times, the accepted terms version and when it was accepted, and the fact that you meet the age limit together with the version of the age policy used. Your date of birth is neither sent nor stored; it is only checked on your device.
  • Security: request receipts and rate limits to prevent misuse and duplicate requests, and server error and security logs without nicknames or sign-in tokens.
  • On your device: the sign-in session. On Android it is stored encrypted in the device keystore and is excluded from backups; in a browser, Firebase manages the session. Signing out removes the session.

Providing the account is based on contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): we need this data to create the account, sign you in and manage your nickname. Security data is based on our legitimate interest in protecting the service and its users (Art. 6(1)(f)). Account data is not used for advertising or profiling, and playtest telemetry is not linked to the account. Your nickname is not currently public; before World Rank opens, we will explain what the leaderboard shows.

The account database and server run on Google Cloud in the europe-west1 region (Belgium). Firebase Authentication also processes data in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, under which Google is certified, and on the standard contractual clauses in the Google Cloud data processing terms.

Account data is kept for as long as the account exists. You can delete your account at any time at worldtour.exsports.fi/delete-account or by email; see the deletion guide. A deletion request blocks the account immediately. Our account data and the Firebase Authentication user are deleted as soon as possible and within 30 days at the latest. The nickname is detached from the account immediately, and its comparison form stays reserved for 30 days so that nobody can take over the name right away. Receipts and rate limits are removed within 30 days at the latest. No backups or exports are taken of the account database, so deleted data does not remain in them; if backups are introduced later, deletion will cover them before they are enabled and this notice will be updated.

Google states that it deletes Firebase Authentication data from its internal systems and backups within 180 days of deletion and keeps IP addresses in logs for a few weeks (Firebase privacy information). Deleting your account does not delete your Google account or the game progress stored on your device.
