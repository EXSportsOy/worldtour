Draft World Rank rules · 23 October 2026. World Rank opens with Season 1 on 1 December 2026, and the season ends on 1 March 2027. There are no weekly competitions at launch, and they are not part of the purchase. These rules will be completed before real submissions open.

1. Participation

World Rank is an optional online feature of the full version of the game. Taking part requires the Unlock the full game one-time purchase, a free player account (Google sign-in, age 16 or over) and a public nickname. The same age limit of 16 applies to the whole game.

The submission route is the official Android game installed through Google Play. Each person uses one player account. Do not use another person's account or evade a ban with a new account. There is no separate entry fee for the season ranking, because entry is included in the full version. Any other purchase requirement will always be disclosed before entry.

2. Nicknames and respect

Use an invented nickname, not your full name, contact details or someone else's personal information. Hate speech, threats, sexual harassment, discriminatory or otherwise abusive names, impersonating staff or another person, and misleading advertising are prohibited. Altered spelling does not make a prohibited name acceptable.

Length, allowed characters and change limits are shown when choosing a nickname. Do not publish content you have no right to use.

3. Fair play and verification

Submit only runs you played yourself. Modified game builds, forged scores or recordings, someone else's run, input-generating bots or macros, and disruption are prohibited. Ordinary accessibility assistance is allowed provided it does not automate play or provide an advantage prohibited by the rules.

The server recalculates every score by replaying the recorded inputs. Invalid or technically unverifiable submissions may be rejected. Score verification alone does not prove who played or whether automation was used. You may ask for an explanation and human review of a rejection.

4. Seasons and weekly competitions

Before a competition begins, we state its opening and closing times and timezone, track and game version, scoring, tie-break method, permitted submissions and handling of technical interruptions. Unless otherwise stated, it is a ranking competition with no cash or physical prizes.

Any prize competition will have separate rules published before entry, identifying the organiser, eligibility and territory, prizes, winner selection and contact, and data processing. This draft alone does not launch a prize competition.

Test-season scores do not become production-season records. A new season may start with an empty leaderboard; any archive and retention policy will be explained separately. Changes to physics or scoring may require separate result categories. A technical interruption does not justify arbitrary mid-competition rule changes.

5. Report a problem

You can report a problem in the game or by email. In the game, open the World Rank list, select another player's row and choose Report / Block.

By email, write to info@exsports.fi. Include the result URL or nickname, the content you believe breaches a rule or law and why. Where needed, provide contact details for follow-up and confirm that you are reporting to the best of your knowledge. Do not publicly spread a suspected personal data leak.

EXSports reviews reports within seven days, and urgent safety risks sooner. This is a handling target, not a promise of a particular outcome.

6. Moderation and review

Action depends on seriousness, repetition and impact. Options include correcting a name, hiding or removing content, rejecting a score, and temporary or, in serious cases, permanent online restrictions. The number of reports alone does not establish a violation.

We explain the decision, reason and available review route unless law or a justified security need prevents disclosure of particular information. Request reconsideration at info@exsports.fi; the reviewer will consider your account of events. We do not impose a private deadline that limits statutory appeal or dispute rights.

7. Public content and recordings

Before submission, you will be shown what becomes public. Only your nickname, official score and placement are public, and they can be visible without sign-in. Input recordings are not published, and the game has no videos. This does not grant EXSports a blanket right to feature you or your run in advertising.

Request deletion using the deletion instructions. Also see the terms of use and privacy notice. Organiser: EXSports Oy, company details.
