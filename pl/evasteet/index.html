Website cookies and technical information · updated 1 October 2026. This describes the current worldtour.exsports.fi website. Game test data is covered separately in the privacy notice.

1. What the site stores in your browser

EXSports does not set cookies on this site or use browser local storage to track visitors. The site has no advertising pixels, analytics service, sign-in, third-party embedded videos or externally loaded fonts.

The language of the marketing pages is selected through the page address, your previous language choice or your browser's language settings. If no supported language is found, English is used. Your language choice is stored in browser local storage under worldtour-language until you change it or clear the site's data in your browser. The stored value is only a language code and is not used for tracking. Legal documents use the language selected through their address. The “Copy link” button copies the displayed link to your clipboard only when pressed. Your browser may retain ordinary cached files to make pages load faster.

2. Connections and server logs

The site is delivered through GitHub Pages. Connections transmit technical request information, and GitHub states that it logs visitor IP addresses for security. Having no cookies therefore does not mean that no personal data is processed. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. External links

Links to Google Play or the company's other sites take you to services governed by their own practices. An email link opens your chosen email application. Simply visiting this website does not send a support message.

4. Future changes

If non-essential cookies or similar tracking are added, we will explain their purposes, providers and retention and obtain any required consent before use. Refusing and withdrawing consent must be easy. No tracking-cookie choice is needed now because that tracking is not present.

Questions: info@exsports.fi. The site provider is EXSports Oy; company details.
